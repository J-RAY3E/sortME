# Skill: difficulty levels

Calibrate `level` on what a reader must already know:

- 1: introductory explainer, visual guide, or first-week course material.
- 2: practical blog post or tutorial that assumes basic ML knowledge.
- 3: standard paper or engineering guide requiring familiarity with the topic.
- 4: specialized technique, benchmark study, or advanced training details.
- 5: research frontier, theoretical result, or novel method.

Use the full range. Do not assign 3 to everything; the learning path depends on
a meaningful spread.
